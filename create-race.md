## Overview

To add a new race for a class, click the ![Add.png](images/add.png) icon. If the ![Add.png](images/add.png) icon is in the Race 10 column, this will create a new Race 10 and the ![Add.png](images/add.png) icon will move to the Race 11 column.

![Add-new-race.png](images/add-new-race.png)

The system wil then take you to the edita race page. A new race 10 will be created.

![Edit-new-race.png](images/edit-new-race.png)
